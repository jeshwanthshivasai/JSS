# Awwwards Jury Standards

To achieve SOTD (Site of the Day), a project must excel in four key quadrants. Use these instructions to evaluate and refine all work.

## 1. Design (40%)
The visual language must be cohesive, impactful, and innovative.
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Does the eye move intuitively from the most important element to the least?
- **Typography**: Is there a clear contrast between headings (Display) and body text? Avoid default font spacings; use intentional kerning and line-height.
- **Grids & Layout**: Use broken grids or unconventional layouts that still maintain structural integrity.
- **Color Theory**: Avoid generic palettes. Use the `premium_color_theory_palettes/SKILL.md` to establish a sophisticated mood.

## 2. Usability / UI/UX (30%)
A beautiful site that is impossible to navigate will fail.
- **Intuitive Navigation**: Can a user find their way without thinking? (Applying `interaction_design_principles/SKILL.md`).
- **Kinesthetics**: Does the site feel "weighted"? (Applying `advanced_custom_interactions/SKILL.md` for magnetic buttons and custom cursors).
- **Feedback Loops**: Every interaction (click, hover, scroll) should have a visual or auditory response. 

## 3. Creativity (20%)
The "Wow" factor. How does this site push the boundaries of what is possible on the web?
- **Concept**: Is there a strong narrative or "hook"? (e.g., the "Architect's Pivot" storyline).
- **Originality**: Does it look like an existing template, or does it feel like a custom-crafted experience?
- **Animations**: Use `motion_and_transitions/SKILL.md` to ensure animations are choreographed, not just random.

## 4. Content (10%)
Information architecture and copywriting.
- **Tone of Voice**: Is the copy consistent with the brand? (Applying `creative_writing/SKILL.md`).
- **Readability**: Is the content easy to digest on all devices?

## 5. Technology (Bonus/Technical Polish)
- **Performance**: High frame rates (60fps+) even with WebGL. (Check `extreme_performance_auditing/SKILL.md`).
- **Responsiveness**: The "mobile experience" is often where Awwwards winners are decided. The site must be fluid.

## Implementation Guidelines
When a user asks for "Awwwards-level" work, immediately cross-reference this manifesto against the current state of the UI.
